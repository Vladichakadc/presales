Product overview

Cisco® 10G Passive Optical Network (PON) Optical Network Terminal/Optical Network Unit (ONT/ONU) modules enable existing access networks for both business and residential subscribers with 10G PON capabilities. Featuring a standard SFP+ form factor, Cisco 10G PON ONT/ONU modules can be deployed in gateways, switches, routers, and backhaul infrastructure to provide a 10G PON ONT/ONU endpoint.

Cisco XGS-PON ONT SFP+ Module

Compliant with ITU-T G.9807.1, the SFP-10G-ONT20-I module ensures high interoperability, cost-efficiency, and alignment with specific customer requirements. By integrating an XGS-PON MAC and a standards-compliant ONU Management and Control Interface (OMCI) stack, it delivers comprehensive XGS-PON functionality, including robust supervision, monitoring, and maintenance.
