Hardware Installation Guide for Cisco 8400 Series Secure Routers

About this content

Updated: July 25, 2025

Provides step-by-step instructions for installing and setting up Cisco 8400 Series Secure Routers in enterprise environments. It covers pre-installation preparation, physical installation, connection procedures, and guidance for installing and upgrading internal modules and field-replaceable units.

Introduction to Cisco 8400 Series Secure Routers

Outlines hardware features, port configurations, chassis views, ventilation, and product labeling for Cisco 8400 Series Secure Routers, guiding users through platform overviews, supported transceivers, and identification details for secure and efficient operation.

Prepare for Installation

Outlines essential steps and requirements for preparing hardware installation, including safety recommendations, regulatory statements, environmental and rack conditions, power guidelines, console and Ethernet connections, and necessary tools for reliable system setup.

Install and Connect

Outlines the complete process for installing and connecting the router, including unpacking, pre-installation requirements, physical mounting procedures, chassis grounding, and methods for establishing CLI console access on various operating systems.

Install and Upgrade Internal Modules and Field Replaceable Units

Outlines procedures and best practices for installing, removing, and upgrading internal modules and field replaceable units, including memory, power supplies, fan trays, SFPs, and batteries, with safety guidelines and step-by-step instructions for ensuring optimal hardware maintenance and replacement.
