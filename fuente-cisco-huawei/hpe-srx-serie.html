Accelerate threat detection and response with AI

HPE Threat Labs delivers real-time, research-driven threat insights directly to HPE Networking SRX Series Firewalls as security feeds, so you can detect, prevent, and respond to evolving attacks with greater speed, accuracy, and confidence.

Secure your network without slowing down business

Get industry-proven security efficacy without performance trade-offs. Features like firewall policy optimizer, inline traffic inspection, and advanced hardware acceleration provide scalable resilience for high-throughput environments, while supporting business continuity and compliance.

FAQs

What are the key features of HPE Networking SRX Firewalls?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls deliver enterprise grade security with high performance, combining advanced threat prevention, application aware control, secure connectivity (VPN), and built-in resilience, helping businesses protect users and data, ensure uptime, and simplify security operations at scale.
What are the common challenges HPE Networking SRX Firewalls address?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls help organizations protect their campus, branch, data center, and hybrid cloud environments from advanced threats such as ransomware, APTs, phishing, DoS, and insider attacks, while delivering high performance at scale. This ensures customers never have to choose between strong security and fast, reliable connectivity.
Can HPE Networking SRX Firewalls be integrated with other security solutions?

Yes. HPE Networking SRX Firewalls integrate seamlessly with broader security ecosystems, including SIEM/SOC platforms, threat intelligence feeds, endpoint security, and cloud security tools, enabling unified visibility, automated threat response, and consistent policy enforcement across the enterprise.
What are the typical use cases for HPE Networking SRX Firewalls?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls are used to secure campuses, branches, data centers, and cloud environments with a consistent, top-ranked threat prevention efficacy among major firewall vendors. Built on a Hybrid Mesh architecture, SRX firewalls provide unified protection, high availability, and simplified management across distributed environments.
What is the warranty policy for HPE Networkin SRX Firewalls?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls are covered by a standard limited hardware warranty, with software updates, technical support, and advanced replacement services available through Juniper Care Services support contracts. Warranty and support options vary by model and service level chosen.
How do you configure HPE Networking SRX Firewalls?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls are configured through HPE Networking Security Director, our centralized management solution, which uses innovative firewall rule optimization technology to eliminate anomalies while improving efficiency and reducing operational overhead. HPE Networking Security Director also provides intuitive, end-to-end visibility of the entire SRX environment.
Why should enterprises choose HPE Networking SRX Firewalls?

HPE Networking SRX Firewalls bring industry-leading security efficacy, uncompromising performance, and a proven carrier grade heritage directly to the enterprise. The firewalls are powered by Junos OS, the same operating system used by the world's largest service providers, which brings carrier grade reliability, routing, and availability into enterprise environments.
