Best Practices for Configuring Network Tunnels by Device

\r\n
\r\n

You can establish an IPsec (Internet Protocol Security) IKEv2 (Internet Key Exchange, version\r\n 2) tunnel from a network device deployed in your environment to Cisco Secure Access. IPsec\r\n tunnels created for Secure Access accept traffic on all ports and protocols with a throughput\r\n of 1 Gbps per tunnel.

\r\n
\r\n

High availability

\r\n

You must deploy both a primary and a backup tunnel to ensure high availability. If you\r\n configure only a primary tunnel, you will experience interruption of service during regular\r\n maintenance windows or unplanned outages.

\r\n
\r\n
\r\n

Higher throughput

\r\n

If you require more than 1 Gbps of throughput, you can set up multiple tunnels in a network\r\n tunnel group and join them with Equal-Cost Multi-Path (ECMP) routing.
