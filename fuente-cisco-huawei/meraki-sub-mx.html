Overview  

Subscription licensing follows a hardware-agnostic approach, utilizing SKUs that encompass multiple hardware components within a single SKU. The purpose of this document is to outline the specific SKUs associated with the hardware covered under the MX product class. Additionally, this document will emphasize the key features offered by the MX product class, providing valuable insights to our customers.
