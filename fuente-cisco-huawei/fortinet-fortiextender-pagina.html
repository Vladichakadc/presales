Overview

FortiExtender cellular gateways and routers provide secure 5G/LTE connectivity for any deployment. Easily provision 5G or LTE wireless WAN, high availability, and failover, as well as out-of-band management (OOB) and private 5G/LTE. Ruggedized form-factors are available for vehicle, industrial, and outdoor deployments so you can extend your secure network anywhere.

Secure Wireless WAN and Private 5G

Fortinet is a trusted leader in advanced cybersecurity and enterprise networking solutions. Our 5G/LTE products allow organizations to extend, ensure, and secure network edges, transform digital operations with confidence, and deliver optimal stakeholder experiences.

Flexible Management

Choose from a variety of management options to fit your organization’s needs. Manage thousands of FortiExtender 5G/LTE devices and segment your fleets and branches into groups with FortiEdge Cloud (pictured). You can also combine wireless WAN, NGFW, and other branch device management in a single pane of glass with FortiManager.

Optimal 5G/LTE for Any Deployment

FortiExtender cellular gateways and routers come in a variety of form factors to suit any deployment, including branches, campuses, data centers, fleet vehicles, and industrial and outdoor settings. FortiExtender cellular gateways and routers ensure optimal signal reception, minimal attenuation, and the best experience—without the need for expensive, lossy antenna cables. 

Secure Connectivity Beyond the Branch

Fortinet 5G/LTE products extend the Security Fabric not only to branches, but also to other sites where secure 5G/LTE connectivity is needed. FortiExtender Vehicle devices connect and protect mobile fleets to support a variety of deployment scenarios, from logistics to municipal transit, and even first responders.

Introducing FortiExtender Vehicle 511G »

Fortinet's FortiExtender Vehicle 511G (FEV-511G) unified 5G and Wi-Fi to provide secure connectivity from factory to fleet. With 5G eSIM technology, Wi-Fi 6, a 2.5G mGig port, and an IP64 form-factor combined, FortiExtender Vehicle 511Gsatisfies a host of use cases across Wireless WAN, connected fleet, and Private 5G.

Introducing FortiExtender Vehicle 211F »

FortiExtender offers scalable, cost-effective, and resilient 5G and LTE solutions. Driven by Fortinet’s unique approach of security-driven networking, FortiExtender enables business continuity and improved network availability while securing connectivity with wired broadband and cellular networks.

Extending Security to Microbranches with Fortinet Universal SASE »

Fortinet's FortiSASE includes expanded integrations within FortiExtender remote Ethernet gateways to further support organizations securing microbranches and related devices. FortiExtender remote Ethernet gateways intelligently offload traffic from microbranches to a SASE point of presence (POP) for comprehensive security inspection at scale for all devices, including IoT and OT devices. This integration also means FortiExtender remote ethernet gateways can be managed by the same simple, cloud-based management console customers already use for FortiSASE.

FortiExtender Cellular Failover Demo »

FortiExtender provides 5G/LTE Wireless WAN that's fully integrated on the Fortinet Security Fabric. FortiExtender 5G/LTE Wireless WAN gateways come equipped with Dual SIM and Dual Modem capabilities for fast failover and active/active cellular connectivity. In this demo, the Technical Marketing team takes you through our Dual-SIM failover process, also called "SIM-switch." With FortiExtender, you can set failover capabilities based on outages, link quality, and data plan consumption.

FortiExtender Out of Band Management (OBM) Demo »

FortiExtender 5G/LTE Wireless WAN Gateways provide Enterprises secure cellular networking that includes failover and extended WAN links. In this demo, Fortinet Technical Marketing will show FortiExtender Cloud's Out of Band Management capabilities, allowing you to continue to manage branch connectivity in the event of a wired failure.

Parsec Technologies is a US-based company that designs and manufactures high-performance cellular antennas and engineered integrated solutions for cellular (5G/LTE) Wi-Fi and GPS connectivity for enterprise, government, industrial and mission-critical applications worldwide. Built through Fortinet’s Open Fabric Ecosystem, the ECK integrates Fortinet solutions into two Parsec models. The Bloodhound model provides effortless, secure mobile connectivity wherever it’s needed, thanks to a slim form factor. The Pitbull model is built to handle the most demanding mobile needs, supporting the industry’s highest security and networking performance for the best user experience by providing both 5G cellular and satellite connectivity.
