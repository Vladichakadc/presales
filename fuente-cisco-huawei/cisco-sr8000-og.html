Introduction

1.1 Purpose, audience, and scope

This document describes the ordering process for the Cisco® 8000 Series Secure Routers.

Audience: Cisco Public

Scope: This guide describes the ordering for the following products:

●     Cisco 8100 Series Secure Routers

●     Cisco 8200 Series Secure Routers

●     Cisco 8300 Series Secure Routers

●     Cisco 8400 Series Secure Routers

●     Cisco 8500 Series Secure Routers

●     Cisco 8600 Series Secure Routers

For more detailed information on the Cisco 8000 Series Secure Routers, go to www.cisco.com.

Overview

The Cisco 8000 Series Secure Routers deliver the industry’s most complete secure networking experience, combining cutting-edge security, routing, assurance, and SD-WAN capabilities into a unified platform. Designed for diverse use cases—from branches and campuses to data centers— these secure routers empower businesses to adapt dynamically to evolving demands. The routers are powered by Cisco’s System-on-a-Chip (SoC), customized silicon that is built to handle high-bandwidth AI/ML workloads while reducing power consumption and operational costs. This next-generation family of secure WAN routers from Cisco delivers unmatched protection, high-speed performance, and scalability, supporting a multitude of encrypted throughput requirements.

Cisco Software Licenses and Subscriptions

Perpetual OS Software

Each Cisco 8000 Series Secure Router comes preloaded with Cisco Routing OS Essentials, an embedded operating system software that delivers fundamental device functionality. An optional upgrade to OS Advantage perpetual license is also available under respective hardware SKU.

Subscription Software

You can choose a subscription license in addition to hardware with OS Essentials to suit your specific deployment use-case.

●     Cisco Routing Advantage: Offers advanced routing capabilities for a traditional routing deployment.

●     Cisco WAN Essentials/Advantage: Choose between Essentials and Advantage packages for Cisco Catalyst™ SD-WAN deployment.

Note: Cisco Routing Advantage and OS Advantage provide the same license as term and perpetual entitlements respectively.

For a full list of features, visit the Cisco Routing and WAN Licensing Feature Matrix.

Cisco Support

Cisco Support is a suite of AI-powered and expert-led support designed to maximize uptime, performance, and resiliency of your IT environment for the AI era. With three tiers—Basic, Standard, and Signature—each level builds upon the previous one, combining trusted support, proactive insights, and tailored learning resources accessed through Cisco IQ, our unified digital interface. See Service Description for more details.

Cisco Support – Basic

The Cisco Network Subscription includes support benefits consistent with Cisco Support – Basic but excludes any RMA benefits. Each subscription, and devices linked to the subscription, are provided 24x7 TAC access, OS updates, and digital insights. If hardware RMA is needed, Cisco recommends the purchase of an RMA upgrade service level.

Cisco Support for Routing standalone offers

Support services for Cisco Standalone Routing offers, which provide support coverage for the router hardware and associated Routing SW licenses is available for purchase on the router. Cisco Support - Basic and Standard offers can be purchased with the router, providing support coverage of the router, the Cisco Essentials OS SW license as well as the Cisco Advantage term license.

Cisco Support – Standard

Cisco Support - Enhanced is the recommended support offer for the Cisco Networking Subscriptions and Wireless hardware. It includes centralized support for Cisco Solution Alliance Partner products in your infrastructure deployment to resolve complex issues faster, proactive AI insights, and RMA upgrade. This support offer is orderable on the hardware and provides coverage of both the hardware and associated Networking Subscription licenses Essentials and Advantage OS features.

Cisco Support – Signature

Signature is the most valued tier and includes personalized, expert-led support guidance tailored to your precise needs and AI insights for proactive operational excellence. It provides guaranteed restoration Service Level Agreements (SLAs), fastest response time, expert-led predictive disruption analysis and prevention, systematic root cause analysis, vulnerability assessments, asset management, and continuous optimization, certification training, and RMA upgrade.

Cisco RMA upgrade

This advanced replacement hardware service option is available for Cisco Network Subscription products. There are multiple service levels to choose from. The RMA upgrade service, combined with support included in the Cisco Network Subscription provides full alignment to the Cisco Support – Basic service level. See Service Description for more information.
